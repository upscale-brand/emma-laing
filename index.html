About Emma

Nutrition education, research, and media.

Emma Laing, PhD, RDN, LD, FAND, is a clinical professor and director of dietetics at the University of Georgia’s College of Family and Consumer Sciences.

1995–1997

Nutrition and dietetic training

Earned a BS in Nutrition and Dietetics at the University of Delaware, followed by a clinical dietetic internship at Emory University Hospital.

2000–2003

Graduate study at UGA

Completed an MS and PhD in Foods and Nutrition at the University of Georgia.

Today

Teaching and leadership

Leads dietetics education at UGA and contributes to nutrition research, professional service, and national media conversations.
