# Emma Laing site

Prepared from publicly available details on Emma Laing's University of Georgia faculty and press pages and the publicly visible LinkedIn profile. The site uses her name as a temporary wordmark and the University of Georgia-inspired palette. It keeps the source template's section layout, navigation, palette controls, and interaction scripts.

- Name and credentials: Emma Laing, PhD, RDN, LD, FAND.
- Role: Clinical Professor and Director of Dietetics at the University of Georgia College of Family and Consumer Sciences.
- Education: BS Nutrition and Dietetics, University of Delaware (1995); Emory University Hospital clinical dietetic internship (1997); MS Foods and Nutrition (2000) and PhD Foods and Nutrition (2003), University of Georgia.
- Public work contact from UGA: emonk@uga.edu; 706-542-4869; 390 Dawson Hall, 305 Sanford Dr., Athens, GA 30602.
- Areas highlighted: weight-inclusive approaches, intuitive eating, diet culture and weight stigma, nutrition trends and research literacy, bone health, vitamin D, and dietetics education.
- Selected teaching, research, awards, and media descriptions are summarized from UGA pages; the submitted 2027 book chapter is not described as a published work.
- The UGA pages and public LinkedIn snapshot show slight differences in title and credential display. The site follows the UGA faculty bio for title and full credentials.
